Researcher · AI Systems Benchmarking · LLM Inference Evaluation

I am a researcher working on standards and certification at IEIT SYSTEMS Co., Ltd. and a Postdoctoral Researcher at Inspur Group Co., Ltd. (Inspur Group). My current research focuses on AI systems benchmarking, large language model inference evaluation, and the performance and efficiency of server systems. Since 2025, I have also served as the Release Manager for SPEC Machine Learning.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Beijing Institute of Technology in 2024. Previously, my doctoral research focused on computer vision and computational spectral imaging, particularly spectral image compression and reconstruction using physics-based imaging models.
